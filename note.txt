Supplementary Backend Endpoint Additions: Currently, the backend lacks endpoints for updating general task attributes (status, title, description, points, priority, labels, due date) and deleting tasks. To support fully interactive board features like drag-and-drop column changes, in-place title/description edits, subtask status toggling, and deleting tasks, we propose adding:

PUT /api/tasks/{taskId} for updating task details.
DELETE /api/tasks/{taskId} for deleting tasks (with safe cascade deletion of assignments/activities).
